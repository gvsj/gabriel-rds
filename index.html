What Is Bullying?

Bullying is unwanted, aggressive behavior that involves a real or perceived power imbalance. It is repeated over time and can take many forms.

✋

It Is Not Your Fault

No one deserves to be bullied. Bullying says more about the bully than it does about you.

🔁

It Is Repeated

A single negative act is not always bullying. Bullying involves a pattern of behavior targeted at the same individual.

⚖️

Power Imbalance

Bullying involves an imbalance of power — physical, social, or emotional — that the bully exploits.

Types of Bullying

👊

Physical

Hitting, kicking, pushing, taking or damaging belongings.

💬

Verbal

Name-calling, teasing, threatening, or inappropriate comments.

🙅

Social / Relational

Excluding someone, spreading rumors, or manipulating relationships.

📱

Cyberbullying

Harassment via text, social media, email, or other online platforms.

🎯

Prejudicial

Targeting someone because of race, religion, gender identity, or sexual orientation.

😶

Silent / Indirect

Ignoring, giving the "silent treatment," or passive-aggressive behavior.

Effects of Bullying

Bullying can have serious, lasting consequences for everyone involved — the target, the bystander, and even the bully.

For Targets

  • Increased anxiety and depression
  • Lower academic performance
  • Loss of self-esteem
  • Physical symptoms (headaches, sleep problems)
  • Social withdrawal

For Bystanders

  • Feeling helpless or guilty
  • Fear of becoming a target
  • Increased anxiety
  • Desensitization to aggression

For Those Who Bully

  • Higher risk of aggressive behavior later in life
  • Risk of substance use
  • Poor academic outcomes
  • Difficulty maintaining relationships

What You Can Do

1

Speak Up

If it is safe to do so, calmly tell the bully to stop. Your voice has power.

2

Support the Target

Show kindness. Sit with someone who is alone, include them, and let them know they matter.

3

Report It

Tell a trusted adult — teacher, counselor, or parent. Reporting is not "tattling"; it is helping.

4

Document

Save screenshots, messages, or notes that describe incidents with dates and details.

5

Take Care of Yourself

Talk to someone you trust, practice self-care, and seek professional support if needed.
